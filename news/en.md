# Deeply Viewer news

## 2026-11-17 — Deeply Viewer rebranding
Deeply Viewer now uses its own logo, Windows icon, animated login background,
repository, release feed and community branding. It remains an independent
community fork derived from Deeply Viewer and retains the required licenses
and attributions.
[Read more](https://github.com/Diablo85666/Deeply-Viewer)

## 2026-11-17 — Login and graphics defaults
The login screen shows Deeply Viewer news only. A first launch selects the
system language and starts with the Medium graphics profile while preserving
hardware-aware graphics detection and the user's later choices.

## 2026-11-17 — Community features
Deeply Viewer keeps the community improvements for browser access, avatar
controls, texture recovery, performance monitoring, transcript sharing and
verified portable releases.
